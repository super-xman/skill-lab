# Painterly Gouache · 提示词模板

## 中文：图生图（默认 L1）
请将【目标照片】创作为精致但具有**明显油画涂抹笔触**的20世纪中期复古旅行海报水粉插画。仅依据本段文字定义画风，**不要使用额外的风格参考图**；只使用用户目标照片作为构图和主体依据。

保留【人物/建筑/场景的主要识别特征、姿势、道路/岸线、视角、时间、光照与指定颜色】。默认允许适度扩展边缘、补全远景和增加少量合理的场景细节，所有新增元素都必须自然且不抢主体；不得改动人物身份或添加重要情节。

采用 opaque matte gouache + expressive painterly oil-like brush strokes：不透明色块层叠，画布细微颗粒，可见中短程扫涂与断边。**天空能看出有节奏的笔触和色块叠加**，尤其云朵附近，但不可全屏堆积单调斜线；云朵用奶油金、桃橙、柔和灰蓝层层塑造体积。海浪与波光使用连贯的弧线短笔触，山石及道路贴合形体的方向描绘；建筑与人物结构清晰、细节精致。色彩复古，暖光冷影和谐，尊重原图天气与时段。无无关邮轮、灯塔、巨型太阳、文案、水印。

负面：photorealism, CGI, flat vectors, glossy plastic, generic airbrushing, anime, translucent watercolor bleeds, uncontrolled thick impasto, repetitive diagonal scratch marks, deformed faces or buildings, irrelevant story additions, text, watermark。

画幅：【原图比例／用户指定】；扩展级别：【L1 默认；L0 严格；L2 用户明确授权】。

## English
Transform only the target image into a mid-century vintage travel poster painting, with opaque matte gouache and **expressive, moderately heavy painterly oil-brush handling**. No separate style reference image is required or allowed. Preserve recognizable subjects, poses, architecture, perspective, geography, light and scene relationships. Moderate scene-consistent environmental expansion is allowed, but no new protagonists or imagined landmarks.

Use layered opaque color masses, visibly worked brush edges, soft tactile pigment grain, warm golden highlights and balanced cool shadows. Let clouds, sky, waves, rocks and foliage show rhythmic short-to-medium strokes and controlled painterly smearing, while faces and architecture remain precise. The sky is deliberately more visibly brushed than in a refined flat-sky gouache poster, but never a field of chaotic scratches or impasto ridges. Keep the target's weather, time, palette-critical details and aspect ratio. Avoid photographic, CGI, flat vector, anime, grunge, oversaturated neon, invented text and watermark.

- 笔触太乱：「保留绘画性，但清理重复斜线和硬质刮痕；让笔触服从云、波浪、山体结构。」
