# 无参考图的提示词模板 · 中文 / English

以下模板**完全不要求风格图片**。图片编辑时只附目标原图；原创插画只输入文字。默认 L1 适度扩景，保留原本时空和主体。

## A. 中文完整版：目标照片 → 复古旅行海报水粉插画（默认）

> 请把我上传的【目标照片】转换成**精致的20世纪中期欧美复古旅行海报手绘插画**（refined mid-century painted travel poster / editorial illustration），采用**哑光不透明水粉（opaque matte gouache）**。只以目标照片作为主体、构图和场景依据；**不使用、不要求其他任何风格参考图片**。
>
> **内容**：保留【指定主体、身份、姿态、标志性建筑、道路/海岸线、构图、光照与天气】。默认允许适度且合理地延展画面边缘、完善远近景和增加少量符合现场逻辑的细节，但新元素不可改变故事、增加新主角或抢主体。大面积原始留白应保留，不为“丰富画面”而塞满云朵或装饰。
>
> **造型**：像高质量复古商业旅游插画，造型准确、轮廓清晰、建筑透视严谨、细节集中于主要对象；相邻哑光色面自然覆盖，整体整洁精致，有淡淡的旧式印刷色彩关系。不要画成粗放印象派油画。
>
> **天空（强制）**：天空底色采用**几乎平滑的薄层哑光水粉色面**，柔和的自然色调变化、极细且低对比的颜料微颗粒；在正常观看距离**几乎看不到明显笔触**。禁止成排斜向刮痕、油画刀脊、横跨晴空的卷曲笔痕和过度粗糙纸纹。若需要云朵，使用柔软的奶油色块塑造体积，**仅在局部云缘保留细笔触**；原图晴朗时保持开阔天空，不自动增加大片戏剧性云层。
>
> **其他区域**：水面使用方向一致的节奏性短笔触与柔和反光；山石/道路用明暗色块和少量贴合形体的短笔触；树冠可有局部点划；建筑立面和人物保持精细、相对平整。**不同区域笔触密度不同，不全图涂上一样的油画纹理**。
>
> **色彩**：保留原图的时间、光照、天气与主要固有色；用克制的暖金亮部、柔和灰蓝/青绿或相应场景冷色阴影建立复古冷暖平衡；低到中等饱和、温暖而不荧光，避免全画粉紫橙调滤镜。
>
> **禁止**：photo filter, CGI, 3D, plastic surfaces, flat vector, anime cel shading, coarse impressionist oil painting, heavy impasto, thickly painted sky, diagonal rake marks, palette-knife sky ridges, scratched canvas, swirling sky brush strokes, heavy grunge, gritty paper, neon colors, invented text, watermark, irrelevant added objects, changed subject.
>
> 画幅：【沿用原图比例 / 用户指定比例】。扩展强度：【L1适度扩景；按需改为L0严格或L2自由】。

## B. English full prompt (for models biased toward English)

> Transform the **target image only** into an elegant, **refined mid-century vintage travel poster illustration**, rendered in **opaque matte gouache** with the compositional polish of classic editorial commercial art. **Do not use or request any separate style reference image**; the following text defines the complete style.
>
> Preserve the defining subject, recognizable identity, pose, key architecture, geography, perspective, lighting and weather of the target. Allow moderate, scene-consistent environment extension and carefully chosen minor details, but do not add new protagonists, imaginary landmarks or distracting story elements. Respect intentional negative space, especially a wide clear sky.
>
> Build crisp, elegantly simplified shapes with accurate perspective and readable silhouettes. Use layered matte color planes and subtle vintage print-like pigment micrograin, not a photography filter and not loose impressionist oil paint.
>
> **CRITICAL SKY RENDERING:** Paint open sky as **broad, nearly smooth, thin-looking matte gouache color fields** with soft natural tonal shifts and **extremely subtle low-contrast pigment micrograin**. At normal viewing size, the sky should show **virtually no visible directional strokes**. No repeated diagonal hatching, long rake marks, scratched canvas, palette-knife ridges, impasto sky or swirling paint streaks. Any clouds should be softly sculpted from restrained cream/peach color planes, with delicate localized strokes only at cloud edges. Preserve clear-sky negative space; do not invent dramatic cloud walls.
>
> **MATERIAL-SPECIFIC BRUSHWORK:** controlled rhythmic short strokes on waves and reflections; restrained structured short strokes on rocks and dirt roads; selective small dabs in foliage; smooth precise building façades and readable subjects. The sky is much smoother than sea or rocky ground. Do not apply the same thick painterly texture across the whole image.
>
> Use harmonized vintage warm-cool relationships, subdued sky blue, deep teal where appropriate, gentle golden highlights and nuanced cool shadows. Adapt the palette to the original location, season, time of day and subject colors; do not force every scene into a pink-purple sunset.
>
> Avoid photorealism, CGI gloss, plastic sheen, flat vectors, anime cel shading, generic heavy oil impasto, aggressively textured sky, coarse directional scrape marks, gritty paper, neon saturation, unwanted titles, watermarks, copied scenery and subject drift. Maintain the requested aspect ratio. Output a polished standalone illustration.

## C. 新创作，无目标图

## D. 海滨写真专项（与此前“有参考图版本”的目标更接近）

> 保留海岸、海水、酒店外形、棕榈树和步道的空间关系；画成**精致的复古旅行商业插画**。海水以层叠的青蓝与深蓝、少量暖色反光和连贯的短弧形笔触绘制；酒店墙面像平整的奶油色水粉纸面，建筑透视准确。天空维持干净、细腻、哑光的灰蓝大色面，**没有肉眼可见的斜线油画笔痕**；少量云朵只用柔和奶油色块轻度塑造。允许适度增加符合原场景的花木、沿岸细节，不能制造与原图不相干的巨轮、灯塔或戏剧性大太阳。**优先级：海报精致造型 > 哑光色块 > 局部水浪笔触 > 微量纸纹**。

## E. 荒漠写真专项

> 保留荒漠道路、两侧山丘、遥远地平线和渺小独行者的原始空间比例；画成**精致、克制的复古旅行海报水粉插画**。大面积天空保留空旷感，使用极其细腻、平滑的灰蓝至沙金暖色过渡和微弱颜料颗粒，**不要大片斜向笔痕或过度戏剧性的厚涂云层**。远山用灰紫和暖褐色平面层叠，沙地和道路通过短笔触、碎石块面和真实的光影方向表现纹理。人物可辨认但不要擅自变大。只允许少量自然的环境补景；避免把整个画面变成粉橙色重油画。

## F. 局部纠偏：画得太像油画、天空太厚

中文：
> **只修正天空及云朵的颜料质感，不改主体、建筑、山体、海浪与构图。** 抹除天空成排斜向笔痕、划痕、刮刀纹与粗油画堆叠，改成平滑、淡淡颗粒的哑光不透明水粉色面。保持原有天空的色相、光线与留白。云朵仅在边缘保留柔和轻笔触。海水和山石原有细节保持不变。

English:
> **Edit the sky and cloud paint texture only**, keeping subjects, architecture, rocks, water and framing unchanged. Replace diagonal brush scratches, palette-knife ridges and heavy impasto across the sky with smooth matte opaque gouache color fields and faint, even pigment micrograin. Preserve the original hue, natural light and open negative space; limit gentle strokes to cloud edges. Leave non-sky painterly details intact.
